How this works
This page reads events from your Billing calendar and lets you mark each one as Billed or Unbilled. Status is stored in the event’s extendedProperties.private (so it won’t mess up your normal description).
Note: BILLING_ALLOWED_EMAIL is a “soft” block in the browser. If you need real privacy, protect this page with Cloudflare Access (recommended).
